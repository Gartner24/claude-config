# Plain questions (beats skill text)

Every question to the user, in a question panel or in chat, follows this:

- Open with one plain sentence: what is being decided and why it matters to the user.
- Every technical term gets a few plain words right where it is first used
  ("bwrap, a jail that limits what a program can touch"). No unexplained acronyms,
  AC numbers or internal names in the question itself.
- Each option says, in everyday words, what the user gains and what they risk.
  Mark the recommended one and say why in one line.
- When the user picks "Chat about this" or says they do not understand, explain the
  concept from zero with a concrete example, then ask the question again.
- Short sentences. Simple words. Write for a smart person outside the field.
