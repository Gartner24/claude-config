# Hooks for perfume Reels (ES / EN)

The hook is the first 1 to 3 seconds: the opening frame, the first spoken line and the first on-screen text. On
Instagram most viewers decide in that window whether to keep watching (see SKILL.md "Platform facts" for sources).

## The hook gate (check every hook before export)

Adapted from the WeAreNoCode YouTube editor's hook gate (MIT, see `scripts/LICENSE-MIT-starter-kit.txt`):

1. **Frame 0 is a meaningful image**, not a settling camera, a black frame or you taking a breath. Bottle in hand,
   spray mid-air, your face mid-sentence, or the product PNG already on screen.
2. **It works muted.** The first text card states the promise on its own (a large share of Reels are watched with
   sound off, and captions are the only way they get the hook).
3. **The first spoken word survives.** No cut, SFX peak or card entrance lands on top of it. Text arrives on or just
   before the word (0.10 to 0.25 s ahead), never after it.
4. **The promise has a payoff time.** Write it down: "hook asks 'is it worth 180 USD?' at 0.4 s, answered at 41 s".
   Close the promise before any call to action ("sígueme", "link in bio").
5. **One open loop at a time** (one main question, one secondary at most).
6. **No cut in the hook take.** Your projects keep the first 1.6 to 4.2 s as one take (median 2.8 s) and carry energy
   with a title cascade and a 2-step zoom instead. Keep that: it reads as confident.
7. **Text first, picture payoff second.** Your data: title chunk at 0.0 s, first PNG / b-roll payoff at 2.3 to 3.0 s.

## Structure of a 3-second hook (your CapCut pattern)

| t (s) | What lands | How |
|---|---|---|
| 0.00 | lead-in words (white, Montserrat Bold/Light, small) | "Aparición progresiva" 0.13 s, Pop SFX |
| 0.20 to 0.50 | second chunk | same, staggered |
| 0.70 to 1.10 | the key word (Scholar, neon + cream, glow) | "Tipo 2" 0.13 s, finger-snap SFX |
| 1.40 to 1.60 | the closing chunk (or the price / rating) | "Aparición progresiva" 0.13 s |
| 2.30 to 3.00 | the visual payoff: bottle PNG cutout or b-roll | 0.5 s fade in, "Caramelo explosivo" |
| 0 to 4.5 | zoom template under it all: 100 -> 102.6 -> 106.4 -> 100 % | keyframes at 0 / 1.27 / 2.27 / 4.5 s |

Total hook copy: **3 to 9 words on screen**, split into 2 to 4 chunks of 1 to 4 words.

## Formulas

Each formula: what it does, then examples. Use the language of the video; for bilingual accounts, keep the spoken line
in one language and the on-screen key word can be in the other only if it is a brand or a product name.

### 1. Curiosity gap (an incomplete statement)
Opens a question the video answers.
- ES: "Nadie habla de este perfume…" / "El perfume que más cumplidos me ha dado no es el que crees" / "Lo compré por
  error y ahora es mi favorito"
- EN: "Nobody talks about this one…" / "My most complimented perfume isn't what you think" / "I bought it by mistake.
  Now it's my signature."
- Payoff: name and show the bottle within 3 to 8 s; do not hold the reveal to the end in a 30 s Reel.

### 2. Contrast / expectation flip
Two things the viewer did not expect together.
- ES: "Huele a 300 dólares. Cuesta 30." / "Barato, pero no huele barato" / "Un perfume árabe que huele a nicho francés"
- EN: "Smells like $300. Costs $30." / "Cheap, but it doesn't smell cheap" / "A Middle-Eastern clone that out-performs
  the original"
- On screen: the two numbers or words in your two colours (neon for the surprise word, cream for the rest).

### 3. "Si te gusta X…" (if you like X, you'll love Y)
Targets an existing audience by a famous reference. Strong for saves and sends ("this is for you").
- ES: "Si te gusta Baccarat Rouge 540, tienes que oler esto" / "Si amas el olor a vainilla, guarda este video" / "Si te
  encanta Aventus pero no quieres pagar 400 dólares…"
- EN: "If you love BR540, you need to smell this" / "If vanilla is your thing, save this" / "If you like Aventus but not
  the price tag…"
- Show the reference bottle PNG on the left, the new one on the right (your 3-slot / pair layout).

### 4. Compliments / social proof
The most-searched benefit in the niche.
- ES: "El perfume por el que más me preguntan" / "3 personas me pararon en la calle por este perfume" / "Mi novia no
  deja de olerme cuando lo uso"
- EN: "The perfume strangers stop me for" / "Three compliments in one day" / "Compliment monster, under $50"
- Only claim compliments you actually got; keep it specific (a number, a place).

### 5. Dupe / clone
- ES: "El mejor dupe de Delina que he probado" / "¿Clon o original? Te reto a adivinar" / "Este dupe huele MÁS fuerte
  que el original"
- EN: "The best Delina dupe I've tried" / "Clone or original? Guess before I tell you" / "This dupe projects harder
  than the real one"
- Pair with a split screen: original PNG vs dupe PNG, price under each. Name the brand you compare to only as a
  reference; avoid implying the clone is the original.

### 6. Season / occasion / weather
Instagram search and saves love utility ("what should I wear for…").
- ES: "3 perfumes para el otoño que huelen a suéter calientito" / "Perfume para una primera cita" / "Lo que uso cuando
  hace 35 grados"
- EN: "3 fall perfumes that smell like a cosy sweater" / "First-date perfume, no risk" / "What I wear when it's 95°F"
- Ties naturally to a list format: one bottle per beat, numbered.

### 7. Ranking / number promise
- ES: "Top 5 perfumes de Creed, del peor al mejor" / "Probé 10 vainillas, solo 2 valen la pena"
- EN: "Every Creed I own, ranked" / "I tried 10 vanillas. Only 2 are worth it."
- The number appears in the first card; a progress mark (1/5, 2/5…) keeps the loop open. Your ranking edits run
  ~1 overlay per second; keep that energy but respect the safe zones (see text-design.md).

### 8. Bold opinion / hot take
- ES: "Opinión impopular: Sauvage ya no huele bien" / "Este perfume está sobrevalorado y te digo por qué"
- EN: "Unpopular opinion: this hype perfume is boring" / "Overrated. Here's why."
- Drives comments. Back it with a reason within 5 s, or it reads as bait.

### 9. Blind reaction / first impression
- ES: "Lo huelo por primera vez en cámara" / "Mi reacción honesta a…"
- EN: "First sniff, on camera, no edits" / "Honest first impression"
- Frame 0 = the spray or the face mid-reaction; the cap coming off is the open loop.

### 10. Price / value shock
- ES: "180 dólares por 100 ml. ¿Vale la pena?" / "El perfume más caro que tengo"
- EN: "$180 for 100 ml. Worth it?" / "The most expensive bottle I own"
- Uses your "price" support-text preset (white Montserrat Bold, two lines).

### 11. Direct address / identity
- ES: "Hombres que quieren oler caro: esto es para ustedes" / "Si trabajas en oficina, guarda esto"
- EN: "Guys who want to smell expensive, this is for you" / "Office-safe perfumes, save this"

### 12. Event / place / story (your trip videos)
- ES: "Estoy en el Fairmont y esto es lo que huelo" / "Fui a la tienda de perfumes más famosa de Dubái"
- EN: "I'm at the Fairmont, here's what I'm wearing" / "Inside the most famous perfume shop in Dubai"
- Open on the place, not your face; the face comes in on the cut.

## Words that work as the neon key word

Short (1 to 2 words), concrete, sensory or emotional: CUMPLIDOS / COMPLIMENTS, DUPE, 10/10, VAINILLA, OTOÑO / FALL,
BARATO / CHEAP, LUJO / LUXURY, ADICTIVO / ADDICTIVE, BESTIA / BEAST MODE, SOBREVALORADO / OVERRATED, the brand or
perfume name, a price, a number.

## What to avoid

- Greetings as the hook ("Hola chicos, hoy les traigo…"). Put the greeting after the promise, or cut it.
- Logos, intros, title cards without a claim, or a slow push-in on an empty shelf as the first frame.
- A hook text that just repeats the caption word for word; the text should compress the line, not transcribe it.
- Bait you do not pay off ("you won't believe…" with nothing surprising). Instagram's ranking reportedly weighs
  watch time and sends; bait that loses viewers at 5 s hurts more than a plain hook.
- Text that covers the bottle or your face in the hook frame.
- A whoosh under hook text (the YouTube kit's most common mistake). Your Pop / Click SFX are fine; keep one SFX per
  text chunk, not one per word, and at most one swoosh per picture payoff.

## Writing the hook (process)

1. Write 3 hook candidates from 3 different formulas.
2. Read each muted: does the text alone say the promise? Is it 9 words or fewer?
3. Pick the one whose payoff is the strongest moment in the footage; note the payoff time.
4. Say it in one take; cut nothing in the first 2 to 3 s.
5. Optional: post a second version as an Instagram Trial Reel (shown to non-followers first) to test the hook, if the
   feature is available on the account (see SKILL.md).
