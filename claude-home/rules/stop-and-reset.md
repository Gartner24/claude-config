# Stop and reset (beats skill text)

Source: the JSM course's "Recover a Broken Session" lesson: "after two failed root-cause guesses re-sort", then reset the session (jsm-course-gated-research.md, section 2, item 2).

- After 2 failed attempts to fix the same problem, stop patching. No third patch on the same guess.
- Say in plain words what was tried and what was learned.
- Then start again from a clean state: a fresh look at the cause from the evidence, or a fresh chat that carries a short note of what was learned (what we build, what went wrong, what to avoid, where to restart).
- Put the note in the feature's brief, never only in chat (`CLAUDE.md` Context: brief before handoff).
